Run `python3 studio/studio.py film resume -p $ARGUMENTS` (removes the pause flag and starts the background runner; it
recovers interrupted jobs and continues from the engine state). Then `python3 studio/studio.py film status -p $ARGUMENTS`
and summarise in Egyptian Arabic: current stage, finished milestones, costs, and anything that needs the human.
