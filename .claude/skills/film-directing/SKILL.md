# Film Directing

Prerequisite: GATE-03 APPROVED. Output: a *Director's Plan* section in VISUAL_STYLE_GUIDE.md plus per-scene
notes in `reviews/DIRECTORS_PLAN.md` (create if absent, free form). English.

## Include
1. **Visual language** — one governing idea per film and per act: lens family, depth of field, camera
   height, handheld vs locked, colour/contrast arc, recurring motifs. Photorealistic live-action look.
2. **Per scene (SC-ids)**: intent (emotion/turn), geography & screen direction (who is left/right, where
   the enemy is, 180° line), blocking, key beats in order, camera strategy, movement vocabulary, pacing
   (target seconds), transitions in/out, sound/silence intent for the sound-director.
3. **Continuity bible for the director** — time of day, weather, light direction, costume state and wounds
   progression, weapon/prop state by scene.
4. **AI-feasibility pass** — prefer shots a video model can hold: 3–6 s, one main action, ≤3 visible
   characters, motion simple and forward; handle large battles with wides, silhouettes, dust, cutaways.
5. Open creative questions for the human. Never change the approved script; propose changes via the
   Executive Producer.

Handoff: storyboard-planning consumes this plan.
