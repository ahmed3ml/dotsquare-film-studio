# Story Development

Prerequisite: GATE-01 APPROVED (HISTORICAL_BIBLE.md). Output: `STORY_TREATMENT.md` (GATE-02).
Write in **English**; report to the human in Arabic.

## Steps
1. Read PROJECT.md (runtime, genre, protagonist, constraints) and the approved HISTORICAL_BIBLE.md.
2. Offer 2–3 logline options with different dramatic angles; recommend one. Do not choose silently —
   the chosen angle is part of the treatment the human approves.
3. Structure for the runtime: beats with time budget (`SC-001…`, seconds each) summing to the runtime
   (±5%). Short film (≤10 min): max ~6–9 scenes, one protagonist question, one turning point, one image
   that resolves it.
4. Character arcs for each principal (CH-ids provisional): want, need, flaw, change. Keep the protagonist
   central while representing preceding commanders/figures accurately per the bible.
5. Every beat carries a **fidelity tag**: `HC-xxx` (grounded in an ESTABLISHED claim), `DISPUTED:HC-xxx` (which
   version chosen and why) or `DRAMATIZATION` (invented; justify).
6. Feasibility notes for AI production: crowd size limits, number of recurring faces, complex
   interactions to avoid or stage in wide/silhouette.
7. List open questions for the client. Never resolve a historical conflict by invention.

## Done when
Treatment sums to runtime, every beat tagged, no `[[TBD]]`, script-review/historical check by the Historical
Researcher done. Executive Producer submits GATE-02.
